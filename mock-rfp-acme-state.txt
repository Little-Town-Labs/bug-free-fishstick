SECTION 1: INTRODUCTION AND BACKGROUND
================================================================================

1.1 Purpose

The State of Acme, Department of Administrative Services (hereinafter "the State" or
"the Agency") is soliciting proposals from qualified vendors to design, develop, and
implement an Automated RFP Processing and Response Management System. The State
currently receives approximately 200–300 RFP documents per year from various federal,
state, and municipal entities and seeks to modernize its response capabilities through
artificial intelligence and workflow automation.

1.2 Background

The State of Acme's procurement division currently processes incoming RFPs using a
manual workflow. Staff members read each RFP in full, extract key requirements,
research appropriate response content, draft responses across multiple departments,
coordinate approvals, and submit final documents. This process is resource-intensive,
prone to inconsistency, and results in missed opportunities due to tight submission
deadlines.

The Agency seeks a software solution that leverages modern AI/ML technologies to:
  - Automatically ingest and parse incoming RFP documents (PDF, Word, Excel)
  - Extract and categorize requirements, evaluation criteria, and deadlines
  - Generate draft responses based on historical winning proposals and company knowledge
  - Route response sections to appropriate subject matter experts for review
  - Track approval workflows and submission deadlines
  - Maintain a searchable knowledge base of past proposals and responses

1.3 Current State

  - 12 FTE staff dedicated to RFP processing
  - Average response time: 14 business days per RFP
  - Current win rate: approximately 34%
  - Document storage: shared network drive (unstructured)
  - No systematic tracking of lessons learned or win/loss analysis

SECTION 2: SCOPE OF WORK
================================================================================

2.1 Required Capabilities

The vendor shall provide a fully functional system that includes, at minimum:

2.1.1 Document Ingestion and Parsing
  a) Support for PDF, Microsoft Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx), and plain text formats
  b) Automatic extraction of: deadline dates, submission requirements, evaluation
     criteria, mandatory certifications, page limits, formatting requirements
  c) OCR capability for scanned documents
  d) Batch processing of multiple documents

2.1.2 AI-Powered Response Generation
  a) Natural language generation for draft response sections
  b) Integration with agency knowledge base (existing proposal library)
  c) Compliance checking against solicitation requirements
  d) Confidence scoring for generated content
  e) Citation of source materials used in response generation

2.1.3 Workflow Management
  a) Configurable approval workflows with role-based access control
  b) Automated routing to subject matter experts by topic area
  c) Deadline tracking and automated reminders
  d) Version control and audit trail for all document changes
  e) Return-for-revision workflow with comment tracking

2.1.4 Knowledge Base Management
  a) Structured repository of approved response content
  b) Tagging and categorization by topic, agency type, contract value
  c) Automatic extraction of learnings from completed proposals
  d) Search functionality across all historical proposals

2.1.5 Reporting and Analytics
  a) Win/loss tracking and analysis
  b) Time-to-completion metrics
  c) Staff utilization reporting
  d) Proposal pipeline dashboard

2.2 Non-Functional Requirements

  a) Web-based application accessible via modern browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Edge)
  b) Role-based access control with SSO integration (SAML 2.0 or OAuth 2.0)
  c) 99.5% uptime SLA during business hours (7 AM – 7 PM EST, M–F)
  d) Data encryption at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.2+)
  e) SOC 2 Type II certification or equivalent
  f) Compliance with State of Acme data residency requirements (data must remain
     within the continental United States)
  g) API access for integration with existing systems (ERP, document management)
  h) Mobile-responsive design
  i) WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility compliance
