## Core Services and Legacy-to-Cloud Migration Expertise

> *Source: Content Library — [Services] Managed Services Overview; [Services] Professional Services Engagement Model; [Standards] Coding & Development Standards; [Standards] Data Governance Standards*

Our firm is a Software Services and Managed Services company delivering end-to-end technology solutions across the full application lifecycle. Our core service lines include Professional Services (application development, systems integration, and cloud migration), Managed Services (24/7 monitoring, patch management, incident response, and performance optimization), and Data & Automation Services (pipeline engineering, workflow automation, and analytics enablement). Together, these capabilities position us as a single, accountable delivery partner for organizations undertaking complex enterprise system modernization initiatives such as the one described in this RFP.

**Legacy-to-Cloud Migration Expertise**

Our approach to legacy-to-cloud migrations is grounded in a structured, four-phase Professional Services Engagement Model that has been refined across numerous enterprise engagements:

1. **Discovery & Assessment** — We begin every engagement with a rigorous current-state analysis. This includes stakeholder interviews, existing architecture documentation review, a formal requirements workshop, and the development of a risk register. For legacy replacement initiatives, this phase produces a detailed application portfolio assessment, dependency mapping, and a data readiness report that informs all downstream migration decisions.

2. **Design & Planning** — Our architects produce a target-state solution architecture aligned to the client's cloud platform of choice. This includes integration design patterns, a phased data migration strategy, security and compliance controls mapping, and a fully resourced project plan with clearly defined milestones.

3. **Implementation** — Delivery is executed in Agile sprints with a weekly demo cadence, ensuring continuous visibility and rapid course correction. All implementation work is supported by automated CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure-as-code, and documented operational runbooks. Our development teams adhere to rigorous coding standards — including minimum 80% test coverage, OWASP Top 10 security mitigations, SAST scanning in CI, and OpenAPI 3.1 specifications for all REST endpoints.

4. **Transition & Hypercare** — The final phase includes a structured 4-week hypercare period with elevated support SLAs, knowledge transfer sessions, administrator training, and a formal go-live readiness checklist. All phases include formal sign-off gates before proceeding, ensuring mutual alignment and accountability at every stage.

**Data Governance and Compliance**

Our data handling practices are designed to meet the demands of enterprise environments. All migration work is executed in accordance with our Data Governance Standards, which mandate AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, cloud KMS-based key management, and immutable audit logging for access to Confidential and Restricted data. We maintain compliance with GDPR, CCPA, and applicable state privacy regulations, with quarterly compliance reviews conducted by our Information Security team. For engagements with additional regulatory requirements (e.g., HIPAA, FedRAMP, SOC 2), we engage our compliance specialists during the Discovery phase to ensure all controls are reflected in the solution architecture prior to implementation.

**Managed Services Continuity**

Following go-live, our Managed Services program provides comprehensive operational support, including 24/7 infrastructure and application monitoring with PagerDuty escalation, bi-weekly maintenance windows for patching and updates, quarterly performance optimization reviews, and a dedicated on-call rotation for incident response. All P1 incidents receive a post-incident review within 5 business days. Managed Services contracts are offered on a 12-month term with month-to-month renewal thereafter, providing clients with operational flexibility as their environments stabilize post-migration.

## High-Level Project Schedule

> *Source: Content Library — [Services] Professional Services Engagement Model*

Our proposed project schedule is structured across four phases aligned to the RFP requirements. All phases include formal sign-off gates before the subsequent phase commences, ensuring scope alignment, quality assurance, and client approval at each milestone. The proposed schedule supports the May 1, 2026 project start date.

| Phase | Duration (Weeks) | Estimated Start | Estimated End | Key Deliverables |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1: Discovery** | 3 weeks | Week 1 (May 1, 2026) | Week 3 | Stakeholder interview summaries; current-state architecture documentation; application and data dependency map; data readiness report; risk register; requirements workshop output; Discovery Sign-Off |
| **Phase 2: Data Migration** | 5 weeks | Week 4 | Week 8 | Data classification and governance plan; source-to-target field mapping; migration runbooks; data extraction, transformation, and load (ETL) scripts; data validation and reconciliation reports; migrated data quality sign-off |
| **Phase 3: Automation Setup** | 4 weeks | Week 9 | Week 12 | Workflow automation design specifications; CI/CD pipeline configuration; automated integration test suites (≥80% coverage); operational runbooks; automation sign-off |
| **Phase 4: UAT & Go-Live** | 3 weeks | Week 13 | Week 15 | UAT test plan and test cases; defect log and resolution tracking; go-live readiness checklist; end-user and administrator training sessions; hypercare support activation; production go-live; 30-day hypercare period commences |

**Total Estimated Duration: 15 weeks** (exclusive of 30-day post-go-live hypercare period)

> **Note:** This schedule is based on the assumption that client stakeholders are available within 2 business days for questions and approvals, and that access to source systems, data exports, and required environments is provisioned prior to Phase 1 kickoff. Any material deviations from these assumptions may require schedule adjustment via a formal change request.
