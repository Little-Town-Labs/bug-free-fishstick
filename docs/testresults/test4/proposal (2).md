## Primary Account Manager

> *Source: Content Library — Company Contacts*

Acme Solutions will assign **Priya Patel, VP of Customer Success**, as the primary account manager for the St. Louis Community College (STLCC) account. Ms. Patel brings extensive experience leading customer success organizations at two SaaS unicorns prior to joining Acme Solutions, with a proven focus on long-term partnership value over transactional engagements. Her leadership and client-facing expertise make her ideally suited to oversee the College's account.

**Account Management Approach:**

**Managing Placements:** Ms. Patel will serve as the single point of accountability for all consultant placements under this contract. She will work directly with STLCC's IT leadership to understand each staffing request's technical requirements, urgency level, and desired skill profile. Upon receiving a request, she will match available consultants from our bench based on their certifications, experience, and product-specific qualifications. She will present candidate profiles to STLCC for review and approval before any placement begins, ensuring alignment with the College's expectations.

**Supervising Overall Account Performance:** Ms. Patel will implement a structured performance management framework that includes:

- **Weekly Status Reports** — Summarizing active placements, hours worked, tasks completed, and any open issues.
- **Monthly Performance Reviews** — Formal reviews with STLCC management to evaluate consultant performance against established KPIs, including timeliness of service delivery, quality of work, responsiveness, and adherence to College policies.
- **Quarterly Business Reviews (QBRs)** — Strategic sessions with College leadership to review overall account health, discuss upcoming technology initiatives, assess staffing forecasts, and identify opportunities for improvement.
- **Consultant Performance Scorecards** — Each consultant will be evaluated on technical competency, professionalism, communication, and adherence to deadlines. Underperforming consultants will be placed on a corrective action plan or replaced promptly.

**Interface with College Management Staff:** Ms. Patel will establish a clear communication cadence and escalation framework from day one. She will be accessible via phone, email, and video conferencing during business hours and will respond to urgent communications within two hours. She will maintain a standing weekly check-in meeting with the College's designated IT point of contact and will be available for ad hoc meetings as needed. An escalation path will be documented so that any concerns not resolved within 24 hours are escalated to Acme Solutions' executive leadership, including CEO Sarah Chen, if necessary.

**Supporting Contact:** James O'Brien, VP of Sales & Partnerships, will serve as the secondary point of contact for contractual and commercial matters. Mr. O'Brien can be reached for any contract-related inquiries and brings 15 years of B2B enterprise sales experience to support the account relationship.

## Travel Reimbursement Policy

> *Source: No knowledge base match — consider uploading relevant content*

Acme Solutions maintains its headquarters at 123 ABC Lane, St. Louis, MO 63105, which provides us with the distinct advantage of local presence for servicing STLCC campuses throughout the St. Louis metropolitan area. Our travel reimbursement policy is as follows:

**Local Consultants (St. Louis Metropolitan Area):**
- Consultants residing within the St. Louis metropolitan area will not incur travel charges to the College. Travel to and from STLCC campuses is considered part of the consultant's normal commute and is absorbed by Acme Solutions.
- No mileage, tolls, or parking fees will be billed to the College for local consultants traveling between STLCC campus locations during a workday.

**Non-Local Consultants:**
- In situations where a specialized consultant from outside the St. Louis metropolitan area is required, Acme Solutions will obtain prior written approval from the College before incurring any travel-related expenses.
- Reimbursable travel expenses for non-local consultants, when pre-approved, include:
  - **Airfare:** Economy class, booked at least 14 days in advance when possible.
  - **Lodging:** Up to $150 per night, aligned with GSA per diem rates for the St. Louis area.
  - **Meals:** Up to $60 per day, consistent with GSA per diem guidelines.
  - **Ground Transportation:** Rental car (mid-size or below) or rideshare at actual cost; mileage reimbursed at the current IRS standard mileage rate for personal vehicle use.
- All non-local travel expenses will be documented with receipts and presented on monthly invoices as separate line items for full transparency.

Acme Solutions' commitment is to staff the College's account primarily with local St. Louis-area consultants to minimize or eliminate travel costs.

## Contractor-to-Hire Policy

> *Source: No knowledge base match — consider uploading relevant content*

Acme Solutions recognizes that during the course of a staffing engagement, the College may identify a consultant whose skills and cultural fit make them an excellent candidate for direct College employment. We support such transitions and have established the following policy:

**Conversion Terms:**
- If the College wishes to offer direct employment to an Acme Solutions consultant placed on the STLCC account, a conversion fee will apply. The conversion fee is calculated as follows:
  - **After 0–6 months of placement:** 20% of the consultant's annualized billing rate.
  - **After 6–12 months of placement:** 10% of the consultant's annualized billing rate.
  - **After 12+ months of placement:** No conversion fee. The consultant may transition to College employment at no additional cost.

**Process:**
- The College shall provide Acme Solutions with written notice of its intent to extend an employment offer to a consultant at least 30 days prior to the proposed hire date.
- Acme Solutions will work cooperatively with the College and the consultant to facilitate a smooth transition, including knowledge transfer and replacement staffing if needed.
- There is no restriction preventing the College from making such offers; Acme Solutions views these transitions as a positive reflection of our talent quality and our commitment to the College's long-term success.
