## Professional Services Engagement Model

> *Source: content library — [Services] Managed Services Overview*

Our professional services engagements follow a structured four-phase delivery model to ensure project success, risk mitigation, and knowledge transfer:

### Phase 1: Discovery & Assessment (2–4 weeks)

During the initial phase, we conduct a comprehensive evaluation of your business needs and current state:

- **Stakeholder Interviews** — Sessions with business leaders, technical teams, and end users to understand strategic objectives and pain points
- **Current-State Architecture Review** — Assessment of existing systems, infrastructure, and integration landscape
- **Requirements Workshop** — Facilitated sessions to define functional and non-functional requirements
- **Risk Register Development** — Identification of potential project risks and mitigation strategies

**Deliverables:** Discovery Report, Requirements Specification, Risk Register, Project Charter

### Phase 2: Design & Planning (2–3 weeks)

Our architects and engineers develop a comprehensive blueprint for your solution:

- **Solution Architecture** — High-level design aligned with your business objectives and technical constraints
- **Integration Design** — Detailed integration flows, API specifications, and data mapping
- **Project Plan** — Phased delivery timeline, resource allocation, and milestone schedule
- **Resource Allocation** — Identification of required team members and skill sets

**Deliverables:** Architecture Document, Integration Design Specification, Project Plan, Resource Plan

### Phase 3: Implementation (Duration Varies)

We execute the project using an agile, iterative delivery approach:

- **Agile Sprints** — Two-week sprints with daily standups and sprint reviews
- **Weekly Demo Cadence** — Regular demonstration of completed features to stakeholders
- **Automated CI/CD Pipelines** — Continuous integration and deployment infrastructure for quality and velocity
- **Documented Runbooks** — Operational procedures and troubleshooting guides

**Deliverables:** Working Software, Test Reports, Runbooks, Technical Documentation

### Phase 4: Transition & Hypercare (4 weeks)

We ensure a smooth go-live and establish operational stability:

- **Knowledge Transfer Sessions** — Hands-on training with your operations and support teams
- **Administrator Training** — Detailed training on system administration, configuration, and maintenance
- **Elevated Support SLA** — Enhanced support availability during the critical hypercare period
- **Go-Live Readiness Checklist** — Final validation of all readiness criteria

**Deliverables:** Training Materials, Operations Manual, Support Handbook, Go-Live Report

**Sign-Off Gates:** Formal approval is required before proceeding from each phase to the next.

## Data Governance & Compliance Standards

> *Source: content library — [Standards] Data Governance Standards*

All data handling practices comply with applicable regulations and internal security standards:

### Privacy & Regulatory Compliance

- **GDPR** (European Union)
- **CCPA** (State of California)
- **Applicable State Privacy Laws** (all other jurisdictions)

### Data Retention

- **Personal Identifiable Information (PII)** is retained for no longer than 24 months following relationship termination
- **Purge schedules** are documented and enforced through automated processes

### Data Classification & Controls

All data is classified into one of four tiers, with controls applied accordingly:

| Classification | Definition | Controls |
|---|---|---|
| **Public** | Publicly available, no confidentiality requirement | Standard access controls |
| **Internal** | For company use; not intended for external disclosure | Role-based access; audit logging |
| **Confidential** | Sensitive business or customer data; restricted access | Encryption, MFA, comprehensive audit logging |
| **Restricted** | PII, payment data, regulated data; highest protection | Encryption, role-based access, immutable audit logs |

### Encryption Standards

- **At Rest:** AES-256 encryption for all stored data
- **In Transit:** TLS 1.3 for all network communication
- **Key Management:** Cloud-native KMS (AWS KMS, Azure Key Vault, GCP Cloud KMS) for encryption key lifecycle

### Audit Logging

- **Access Logging:** All access to Confidential and Restricted data logged with timestamp, user identity, and action
- **Retention:** Audit logs retained for minimum 90 days with immutable storage
- **Quarterly Reviews:** Information Security team conducts quarterly compliance audits
